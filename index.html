What is an IDR?

More than a collection of numbers.

An Integrated Diabetes Report is a way to organize relevant diabetes information into a clearer overview. It can help turn separate details into a useful starting point for discussion.

01

Brings information together

See important measurements, history, and care details in one organized view.

02

Shows change over time

Patterns can be easier to discuss when information is viewed in context, not isolation.

03

Supports shared decisions

Use the overview to prepare questions and take part in conversations about care.

Putting the overview to work

A helpful tool for the conversation.

The report is most useful when it reflects your experience and is reviewed with the people involved in your care.

  1. Start with what feels important Note the changes, concerns, or goals you want to bring up.
  2. Look at the full picture together Ask your care team to explain what the information means for you.
  3. Agree on next steps Discuss options and decide what, if anything, you want to do next.

Make room for better questions

Your information. Your questions. A shared view.

Every person's situation is different. Use an IDR as a conversation aid, and ask a qualified healthcare professional about decisions specific to your health.
